# 中文作者的英文病灶

这些不是语法错误，是中文句法搬到英文后的痕迹。审稿人未必指出来，但读起来会「不像这个领域的人写的」。每条给一个改法。

## 1. 主题—评论结构直接搬

中文常说「关于 X，我认为 Y」，英文的主语必须是动作的发出者。

> ❌ As for the mechanism, it is not clear.
> ✅ The mechanism remains unclear.

> ❌ In this study, the main purpose is to explore ...
> ✅ This study asks whether ...

## 2. 长定语前置

中文的「一个能够……的……」在英文里会变成一长串前置修饰。

> ❌ a method that can improve the accuracy of prediction under the condition of limited data
> ✅ We developed a method that improves prediction accuracy when data are limited.

英文习惯先给中心词和动作，再把条件接在后面。

## 3. of 链

> ❌ the improvement of the accuracy of the prediction of the model
> ✅ the model's prediction accuracy improved

一句话里超过两个 `of`，停下来改成主谓。

## 4. 让 research / study 当主语

> ❌ The research thinks that ...
> ✅ We argue that ...
> ✅ The results suggest that ...

`research`、`study`、`paper` 不会「认为」。要么是作者认为，要么是结果提示。

## 5. 时态按节分配

| 节 | 时态 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| Results | 过去时 | We found that X increased Y. |
| Methods | 过去时 | Samples were collected. |
| Discussion（解释） | 现在时 | These results suggest that X limits Y. |
| 普遍事实 | 现在时 | Canopy cover reduces surface temperature. |

中文没有时态，写英文时容易全篇现在时。Results 用现在时，会让审稿人分不清这是本次发现还是已知事实。

## 6. significantly 只用于统计显著

> ❌ The temperature was significantly lower in dense neighbourhoods.（没做检验）
> ✅ Surface temperature was 2.4 °C lower in dense neighbourhoods.
> ✅ Surface temperature was significantly lower (p = 0.003).

没有检验，就不要用 `significantly`。要强调幅度，用具体数字或 `substantially`。

## 7. respectively 顺序必须对得上

> ❌ A and B were 1.8 and 2.4 °C lower, respectively, in dense and sparse areas.
> ✅ Dense areas were 2.4 °C cooler and sparse areas 1.8 °C cooler.

`respectively` 要求前后两个列表顺序严格对应。对不上就不要用。

## 8. 连接词堆叠

> ❌ Moreover, furthermore, in addition, the results also show ...
> ✅ The results also show ...

一个句首最多一个连接词，多数时候一个都不要。顶刊连接词密度 1.7–3.2/千词。

## 9. 名词化 + 空动词

> ❌ We conducted an analysis of the data and made an improvement to the model.
> ✅ We analysed the data and improved the model.

`conduct / make / perform + 名词` 是中文「进行分析」「作出改进」的直译。直接用动词。

## 10. 冠词、单复数、标点

- 可数名词单数前要有冠词：`a model` / `the model`，不是 `model`。
- 泛指用复数：`models`。
- 英文用半角标点，逗号后加空格。
- 不要用中文顿号 `、`；英文用逗号或 `and`。
- `etc.` 尽量不用，列出两项就够。

## 11. In this paper, we ... 开头过多

> ❌ In this paper, we propose a method. In this paper, we also evaluate it.
> ✅ Here we propose a method and evaluate it in three settings.

`Here we ...` 是 Nature 摘要与引言的常用起法，比 `In this paper, we` 短，也更直接。

## 12. 把「填补空白」写成套话

> ❌ This study fills the gap in the literature and provides a robust theoretical basis for future research.
> ✅ Few studies have measured canopy effects in medium-sized cities; we provide estimates from 48 neighbourhoods.

「填补空白」「提供理论基础」在中文刊里常见，在英文顶刊里会被读成没有信息量。改成具体缺什么、你补了什么。

## 一张对照表

| 中文习惯 | 英文病灶 | 改法 |
|---|---|---|
| 关于 X | As for X | X + 动词 |
| 长定语 | 前置从句堆叠 | 主谓先出，条件后置 |
| 的的的 | of 链 | 拆成主谓 |
| 研究表明 | The research thinks | The results suggest |
| 没有时态 | 全篇现在时 | Results 过去，解释现在 |
| 显著 | significantly 滥用 | 给数字或 p 值 |
| 分别 | respectively 错位 | 拆成两句 |
| 首先其次 | Moreover/furthermore 堆叠 | 删 |
| 进行分析 | conduct an analysis | analyse |
| 填补空白 | fill the gap | 写具体缺什么 |
